Questions

What owners usually want to know

Quick answers before you book.

Will I meet you first?

Yes. I offer a free meet and greet so you and your dog can get comfortable before the first walk.

Are you trained and insured?

Yes. I have completed animal welfare, canine first aid, and safe handling training, and I am fully insured.

Can you walk nervous dogs?

Often, yes. I keep things calm and can start with shorter walks if your dog needs time to settle.

How do I pay?

Payment details are confirmed after booking. You can ask about regular walks or one-off walks.
